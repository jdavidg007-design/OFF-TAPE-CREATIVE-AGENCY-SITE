Plan it

Plan your video before you shoot.

Create a storyline with a start, middle, and end.

If you're filming a car video, start with the driver grabbing the keys and starting the engine. Build the energy as they drive off, then finish with them arriving and stepping out.

Your story

Keep it simple. One line each is enough.

Pick your fire moments

A crazy angle, a transition, or a creative camera move.

I plan two or three fire moments before every shoot and add them to my shot list. Some need specific shots, angles or movement. Plan them now so you don't get to the edit and realize you never filmed what you needed.

Shoot it

Shoot variety.

Get wide shots to show the scene, close-ups for details, and different angles to give yourself options in the edit.

Then make sure you get every shot your fire moments need. Take this list to set and check shots off as you go.

Your shot list

Saved on this device. Tap to check off.

OFF-TAPE tip

Capture more variety than you think you need. Before you wrap, check you got every shot for your fire moments.

3

Edit it

Build to the beat drop.

You can choose music anytime. I usually find mine while editing. I look for a song that builds up to a beat drop.

Open with something that grabs attention, then build anticipation as the music rises. When the beat drops, that's where the hype begins.
