The 3 steps

1Plan it

Create a storyline with a start, middle, and end. Driver grabs the keys, drives off, arrives and steps out.

Then plan 2–3 fire moments and add their shots to your shot list.

2Shoot it

Shoot variety. Wide shots for the scene, close-ups for details, different angles for options in the edit.

Get every shot your fire moments need.

3Edit it

Find a song that builds to a beat drop. Open with something eye-catching, build up with the music, hit your fire moments on the drop, keep the energy through the middle, and finish strong.

Add risers for tension, impacts on key moments, whooshes on movement.

I get my sound effects from Epidemic Sound or Ocular Sounds.

3

Fire moments

The shots people rewatch. Each one needs specific footage, so plan it before you film.

Where I find them

  1. Search Instagram.Type the effect or transition into the Instagram search bar and scroll the reels.whip transitioncar transitionsmooth transitionscreative camera movement
  2. Save them to a folder.Tap save on the ones you love and add them to a collection called "Effects & Transitions." I build mine on the go, any time one catches my eye.
  3. Pick from it before you shoot.Open the folder, choose your 2–3 fire moments, and figure out the shots each one needs.
4

Try it today

No car, no client. Just your phone and about 30 minutes.

Find the drop

5 min

Play a song you like. Find where it builds and where the beat drops. Write down the time.

Your whole edit is built around this moment.

Five angles

10 min

Pick any object. Film it wide, close-up, from low, from high, and with one slow push-in.

This is shooting variety.

Whip transition

10 min

End a clip in one room with a fast pan right. Start a clip in another room panning right. Cut where the blur is fastest.

Your first fire moment.

Then cut it togetherBuild up with your close-ups as the music rises, hit your whip right on the drop, and end on your best angle. Post it and tag @j.david007. I'd love to see it.
