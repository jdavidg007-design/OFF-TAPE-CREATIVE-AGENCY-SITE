My process in 3 steps.

Here's one I made.Watch for the build-up, the beat drop, and the fire moments. Then I'll show you how.

1Plan it

Create a story with a start, middle, and end. A car video: the driver grabs the keys, drives off, then arrives and steps out.

Then plan 2–3 fire moments: a crazy angle, a transition, or a creative camera move. Know the shots they need before you film.

Fire moments to try

  • Whip transition: a fast pan hides the cut between two scenes.
  • Lens blackout: push into something dark, pull out somewhere new.
  • Match cut: two shots with the same shape in the same spot.
  • Speed ramp: slow motion that snaps to full speed on the beat.

Where I find them

  1. Search the effect in the Instagram search bar, like "whip transition."
  2. Save the ones you love to a folder called "Effects & Transitions."
  3. Pick 2–3 from it before your shoot.

2Shoot it

Shoot variety. Wide shots to show the scene, close-ups for details, and different angles so you have options in the edit.

Make sure you get every shot your fire moments need.

3Edit it

Find a song that builds to a beat drop. Then build your edit in four parts:

Build-upOpen eye-catching
Beat dropFire moments
MiddleKeep the energy
EndStrong final shot

Add sound design: risers to build tension, impacts on key moments, whooshes on movement. I get mine from Epidemic Sound or Ocular Sounds.
