The 3 steps

My process, every time.
1

Plan it

Plan your video before you shoot. Create a storyline with a start, middle, and end. For a car video: the driver grabs the keys and starts the engine, the energy builds as they drive off, and it finishes with them arriving and stepping out.

Then plan two or three fire moments: a crazy angle, a transition, or a creative camera move. Add the shots they need to your shot list.

2

Shoot it

Shoot variety. Get wide shots to show the scene, close-ups for details, and different angles to give yourself options in the edit. Make sure you get every shot your fire moments need.

3

Edit it

You can choose music anytime. I usually find mine while editing. Look for a song that builds up to a beat drop.

HookGrab attention.
Build-upRise with the music.
Beat dropFire moments, faster cuts.
EndingFinish strong.

Then add sound design:

RisersBuild tension into a big moment.
ImpactsEmphasize key moments.
WhooshesAccent movement and transitions.
3

Fire moments

Effects worth planning for.

Fire moments are what make people rewatch. Most of them need specific shots, so you have to know them before you film, not in the edit.

Browse these, try a couple in Practice, and pick 2–3 for your next shoot. Picked effects add their shots to your plan.

4

Practice

No car, no client. Just your phone.

You don't need a big shoot to get better. Each drill takes about 10 minutes and trains one part of the process. Do them in order and the last one is your first hype edit.
